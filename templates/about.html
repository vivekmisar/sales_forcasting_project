About ForecastFlow

We are on a mission to democratize data analytics. Making professional-grade sales forecasting accessible to everyone.

ForecastFlow started as a simple idea: why should powerful sales analytics be reserved for large enterprises with expensive software? We believed that every business, no matter the size, deserves to understand their data.

What began as a command-line script has evolved into a state-of-the-art web application. Powered by Django and modern web technologies, we process thousands of data points in seconds to give you clarity.
